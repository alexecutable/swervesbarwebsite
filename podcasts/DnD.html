Transform & Roll Out!

ABOUT

Get ready for some Transformers DnD adventures! Using our Homebrewed Transformers DnD book combined with our talented players, stories are brought to life and fun shenanigans are to be had!

Each season a brand new story and continuity, featuring special voice actor guests such as Frank Todaro (Starcream), Jason Marnocha (Megatron), and many more!

SEASON 4

Reforged

Reforge is a story of sparklings navigating their way through Cybertron but not everything is as it seems! They'll be adventures, laughs, tense scenes, and possibly deaths! Follow protoforms Aqua, Bloodstorm, Spyder-Byte, and Radar as they discover who they are and what they want to do while Cybrtron plunges into chaos around them.

Episodes

SEASON 3

Eons

Starting off with the fantastic One-Shot episode starring Linsay Rousseau as Elita-1, this season takes continues the story for Scorchfire Prime, Mantyx, Grease, and Smolder. For the first time in this series this continuity clashes with season 2 characters and more! Our heroes spiral through different timelines, meet variations of characters, and must fight against Galaxywave and Unicron's evil forces! Oh and Starscream is here too I guess, will they survive?

Episodes

SEASON 2

First Stand of The Wreckers

Follow Allegory, Frost Fang, Synthwave, Cross Summit, and Borg who find themselves in the wrong place at the wrong time. They must now solve a mystery to clear their names. A new DM, a new story, new characters, and loads of fun! Let's dive into the mind of Killobyte who takes over as this season's DM and discover what they have in store for our victims... I mean players!

Episodes

SEASON 1

Rise of the World Killers

Join Eightwheeler and Zephyr as they travel through the cosmos trying to find who they are as they meet new faces, such as Scorchfire and Mantyx. All 4 of them with one thing in common, Shockwave. What mysteries lie ahead for our heroes? Tune in to find out.

Special One Shots

From the Shadows

Brace yourselves for fun, adventure, and the usual dose of shenanigans as our players embark on a journey to help the renowned gladiator and upcoming politician Megatron rise to power! In the days preceding the Great War for Cybertron, there is much that needs to be done to sow dissent amongst the citizens...but it must be done covertly. Crosswire, Nightstrike, Steel Back, and Wildcard have been selected to carry out this mission vital to the Decepticon cause, will they be successful? This is...From the Shadows!

Breaking Orbit

A halloween cosmic horror story following a doomed crew and monsters straight out of your nightmares! The victims in this special mini story are Moondust, Moonpool, Hemlock, Moonstalker, Lunesweep, and Dr. Hene.
